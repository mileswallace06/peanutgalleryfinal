Subject: A small local partnership idea for Peanut Gallery

Hi Novel team,

I'm Miles, founder of Peanut Gallery, an Arizona ticket marketplace preparing to launch. I'm putting together a small pilot to encourage fans to list tickets they already own.

Would you consider providing 25 one-use vouchers for a standard scoop? A fan would receive one after we verify their first genuine ticket listing. They would keep it whether or not their ticket sells.

If it's worth discussing, could you let me know which location, item, redemption method and validity period would work? Nothing would be advertised until we agree on the details.

Thanks,
Miles
Peanut Gallery
